MCFT Site Photos — Privacy Policy

Mallet Crafts Private Limited, Pune, India · Effective 22 October 2026

MCFT Site Photos is an internal work tool for the staff and contractors of Mallet Crafts Private Limited ("Mallet Crafts", "we"). It is used to survey and photograph interior-design project sites so that estimates can be prepared and site work tracked. It is not a consumer product and is distributed to invited users only.

What the app collects

  • Site photographs and 360° images you capture with the phone's camera or a connected Insta360 camera, together with the project, room and wall they belong to, measurements you enter, markings you draw, and the time of capture.
  • Voice notes you choose to record (the microphone is used only while you record one).
  • Laser-meter readings received over Bluetooth from a Leica DISTO device you pair. Bluetooth scanning is declared "never for location"; on Android 11 and older, Android itself required the location permission for any Bluetooth scan, and that is its only use. The app does not collect, derive or store your location, and does not location-tag photographs.
  • Your work sign-in: the company server address and the credential you sign in with, stored on the device so the app can sync.

Where it goes

Captured material is stored on your device and uploaded to Mallet Crafts' own project systems — our company ERP and our estimate service — hosted for us by our infrastructure providers. It is used to prepare interior-design estimates, document site conditions and track project work. It is business data of Mallet Crafts' projects, visible to the project team.

The Insta360 camera connection is a direct, device-to-camera Wi-Fi link; images transfer from the camera to the phone locally.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties for their own purposes.

Retention and deletion

Project material is retained as part of Mallet Crafts' project records. To have material about you or your property corrected or deleted, contact us; statutory record-keeping obligations may require keeping some project records.

Children

The app is a workplace tool for adults and is not directed at children.
